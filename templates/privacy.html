01 Who we are

Nkanyezi Academy ("Nkanyezi", "we", "us") operates the Nkanyezi LMS — the learning and attendance management platform used by learners, facilitators, assessors, and administrators participating in SETA-accredited learnership programmes. This policy explains what personal information we collect through the platform, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

This policy applies to anyone using Nkanyezi LMS, including the web attendance tracker, the learner and facilitator dashboards, and the admin portal.

02 What we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the learnership programme and meet SETA reporting obligations:

Identity
Full name, surname, SA ID number, gender, contact number, email address
Programme data
Qualification, enrolment dates, progress, unit completion, assessment scores and feedback
Attendance & location
Sign-in/sign-out timestamps, GPS coordinates at the moment of signing in or out, distance from the registered venue
Account & usage
Login sessions, role and permissions, material views, uploaded documents

We do not collect financial information, biometric data, or any information from your device beyond what's listed above. Location is only ever read at the moment you tap Sign in or Sign out — we do not track your location in the background or at any other time.

03 Why we collect it

  • To run the learnership: enrolling you on the correct qualification, tracking your progress, and recording assessment results.
  • To verify attendance: SETA-accredited learnerships require proof that learners were physically present at the training venue. Sign-in/out times and GPS verification exist to satisfy this requirement.
  • For SETA audit and compliance: attendance and enrolment records form part of the audit trail SETAs require to validate the programme.
  • To support you: facilitators and assessors use your progress and attendance data to flag if you may be falling behind or at risk, so support can be offered early.
  • To administer accounts: login, role-based access, and basic platform security.

04 Geolocation specifically

Because geolocation is the most sensitive data point we collect, here's exactly how it works:

  • Your browser asks your permission before any location is read. You can decline — attendance simply can't be recorded electronically without it.
  • Location is captured only at the instant you sign in or sign out. There is no continuous or background tracking.
  • We compare your coordinates against the registered training venue to confirm you were within range. If you're outside that range, your attendance is still recorded, but flagged as unverified rather than rejected outright.
  • Raw coordinates are stored against your attendance record (for audit purposes) but are only ever shown to you, and to authorised facilitators/assessors/admins, rounded to a low-precision approximation in the interface.

05 Our legal basis (POPIA)

Under POPIA, we process your personal information on the following grounds:

  • Performance of a contract: your enrolment in the learnership programme requires us to process your details to deliver it.
  • Legal obligation: SETAs and related legislation require accurate attendance and enrolment records to be kept.
  • Legitimate interest: identifying at-risk learners early so support can be offered, and maintaining platform security.
  • Consent: specifically for geolocation, which your device only shares once you've actively granted browser permission.

06 Who we share data with

We don't sell personal information. It may be shared, strictly on a need-to-know basis, with:

  • The host employer and SETA associated with your learnership, where required for accreditation, funding, or audit purposes.
  • Facilitators and assessors assigned to your qualification, limited to what they need to support and assess you.
  • Service providers who help us run the platform (for example, cloud hosting and file storage), who are contractually required to protect your data and use it only to provide that service.
  • Regulators or legal authorities, only where we're legally required to disclose information.

07 How long we keep it

Attendance and enrolment records are retained for as long as required by SETA audit rules, even after a learner exits the programme or their account is deactivated. When an account is removed by an administrator, the underlying record is archived rather than deleted, so the audit trail stays intact — this is standard practice for accredited learnerships and is not optional. Outside of that audit requirement, we don't keep personal information longer than necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

08 How we protect it

  • Passwords are hashed and never stored or visible in plain text.
  • Access to learner, facilitator, and admin data is restricted by role — facilitators don't see admin-only data, and learners only see their own records.
  • Session authentication uses secure, HTTP-only cookies, and sensitive endpoints are rate-limited to reduce the risk of abuse.
  • Uploaded materials and documents are stored using access-controlled cloud storage rather than public links.
No system is completely risk-free. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately using the details in section 13.

09 Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, and request a copy of it.
  • Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete.
  • Object to processing that relies on legitimate interest, on reasonable grounds.
  • Withdraw consent for geolocation at any time — by declining or revoking browser location permission — understanding this means attendance can't be recorded electronically for you.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your information has been mishandled.

Requests relating to enrolment, attendance, or SETA audit records may take longer to action where retention is a legal requirement rather than a choice we control.

10 Cookies & sessions

Nkanyezi LMS uses a single essential session cookie to keep you logged in securely. It does not set marketing, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies.

11 Minors

Nkanyezi LMS is intended for learners enrolled in accredited learnership programmes. Where a learner is under 18, processing is carried out in line with the consent and oversight requirements of the relevant learnership agreement and POPIA.

12 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Material changes will be reflected with an updated effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of Nkanyezi LMS after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
